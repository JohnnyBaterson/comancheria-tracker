# Comanchería Tracker

A local-first digital tabletop companion for *Comanchería: The Rise and Fall of the Comanche Empire*.

Open the game in your browser and import your own Tabletop Simulator image folder. The original board artwork is displayed as the playing surface. Your game records and artwork remain in your browser.

Fan-made companion, not affiliated with or endorsed by GMT Games. Game artwork and rules belong to their respective rights holders.
